ABOUT ANCHORAGE

Anchorage 2026 is the premier National Level Techno-Cultural Fest of the Department of Ship Technology, Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), held on October 10–12, 2026 in Kochi.

India is entering a historic era of maritime growth. Driven by national initiatives like Maritime India Vision 2030 (MIV 2030) and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 (MAKV 2047), the future of shipbuilding, offshore engineering, and marine technology depends on one vital resource: elite young talent. Anchorage 2026 is the definitive bridge between today's industry giants and the brightest minds from across the country.

Under the theme "Reimagining the Possibilities," this landmark event bridges fifty years of engineering excellence with transformative maritime innovations. It invites maritime organizations to engage with future professionals, strengthen their presence among the next generation, and contribute meaningfully to the advancement of an industry they continue to lead.

Supported by DOSTAS, the Institution of Naval Architects, and the India Ship Technology Centre (ISTC), Anchorage 2026 is backed by some of the most influential names in India's maritime ecosystem.

Anchorage 2026 reflects the goals of Maritime India Vision 2030 and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 — fostering innovation, indigenous capability, and the skilled talent that will shape India's maritime future.
DEPT OF SHIP TECHNOLOGY
PIONEERS IN NAVAL ARCHITECTURE

The Department of Ship Technology, established in 1974, is a pioneer in Naval Architecture education in India. It aims to develop skilled professionals to meet the needs of the shipbuilding industry, classification societies, R&D organizations, and the Indian Navy.

The department maintains strong collaborations with IITs, foreign universities, shipyards, and industries, enhancing student training and research exposure. Its long-standing partnership with Rostock University, Germany dates back to 1978.

Recognized as a Centre of Excellence by the Indian Navy, the department hosts around 30 Navy cadets in its B.Tech programme. The course is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) under the Tier-I system.

With experienced faculty, over 300 research publications, and programmes including M.Tech (CASAD) and PhD, the department is actively involved in projects with organisations like Cochin Shipyard Ltd., ClassNK, NSTL, and NPOL.

HISTORY LOG
OUR JOURNEY
1974 — FOUNDATION
Department of Ship Technology Established

The Department of Ship Technology, CUSAT, was established as a pioneer in Naval Architecture education in India — building skilled professionals for shipyards, classification societies, R&D organisations, and the Indian Navy.

1989 — GENESIS
Anchorage Is Born

The Society of Naval Architecture Students (SNAS) launched Anchorage — bringing together engineering talent under one roof for the first time. A tradition that would span decades and become one of the oldest festivals of its kind in India.

2000s — EXPANSION
State-Level Recognition

Anchorage grew beyond campus, attracting participants from premier institutions across Kerala and India. It cemented its identity as a techno-cultural and management powerhouse.

2015 — LANDMARK EDITION
Largest Pro Show in Cochin

A landmark edition held on the 9th, 10th and 11th of January 2015. Anchorage hosted the largest Pro Show Cochin had ever seen — a spectacle of music, light and maritime spirit that set an entirely new standard.

2020–2022 — RESILIENCE
Navigating the Storm

The global pandemic tested every institution. Anchorage adapted — embracing hybrid formats, digital workshops and online competitions while keeping the spirit of innovation alive.

2026 — NEW HORIZONS
The Resurrection

Anchorage 2026 returns with the most ambitious programme yet. Scaled-up events, global participants, industry partnerships, the spectacular Navy Expo, and a legendary Pro Show — bridging our 50-year heritage with the future of maritime innovation.

Anchorage 2026 unites the brightest minds from elite national institutions to exchange ideas and drive the future of maritime innovation. A platform where industry meets academia, where cutting-edge maritime technology meets the next generation of decision-makers — an event like no other, a legacy reimagined.
